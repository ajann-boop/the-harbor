⚓ Why The Harbor Exists

Because caregivers face the reality of hearing textbook advice and not having access to communications with people who are actually living through—or have lived through—what they're going through. Since they're not alone in this world, this platform gives them the chance to experience what it feels like to be able to reach out to another caregiver and have that mental support system above all else—because the world changes when you know you're not alone.
