❤️ Dear Guest,
Welcome to The Harbor — Guests, Donors & Sponsors

Thank you for taking a moment to visit. Whether you stopped by out of curiosity, are thinking about becoming a sponsor, or are considering a contribution, we are genuinely grateful you're here.

Our mission is to equip fellow caregivers with the real-world tools and safe harbor they need—whether that means reaching out during a tough hour, venting to someone who truly understands, or taking a breather together through trivia, caregiver bingo, friendly competitions, and community activities.

Directed Giving: Every contribution goes directly toward lifting up caregivers—and if you wish to dedicate a gift to a specific community (for example, directing support specifically toward cancer caregivers or DMD families), your contribution can be filtered and applied strictly for that group.

✨ Contributions go toward ▾
🎯 Caregiver Bingo, Trivia & Activities

Funds digital prizes, caregiver bingo nights, trivia challenges, and friendly community competitions.

☕ Caregiver Respite & Coffee Cards

Sends a $5–$15 coffee or meal e-card to a caregiver pulling an overnight hospital or therapy shift.

🎗️ Dedicated Cause Funds

100% filtered to the specific caregiver group you choose (e.g., Cancer Caregivers, DMD, Autism, or Pediatric).

⚓ Keeping The Harbor 100% Free

Covers hosting, security, and platform tools so caregivers never pay a dime to belong.

Thank you again for taking the time to visit The Harbor and support caregivers walking the storm.
