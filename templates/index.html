🏛️ Caregiver Resources & Foundations
Trusted national agencies, disability foundations, and direct caregiver support links.
🇺🇸 Social Security & National Benefits
Social Security Administration (SSA)
Official portal for Supplemental Security Income (SSI), Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI), and pediatric disability benefit applications.
Visit ssa.gov ↗
Medicaid Home & Community-Based Services
Information on state Medicaid waivers, in-home nursing support, and long-term care services for children and adults with disabilities.
Visit medicaid.gov ↗
USA.gov Disability Services
Central directory for federal & state disability programs, assistive technology grants, and caregiver tax credits.
Visit usa.gov ↗
🎗️ Muscular Dystrophy & Rare Disease Foundations
Muscular Dystrophy Association (MDA)
Care center locator, resource center support, equipment assistance guidance, and neuromuscular family programs.
Visit mda.org ↗
Parent Project Muscular Dystrophy (PPMD)
Dedicated specifically to Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) care guidelines, emergency care cards, family connect groups, and research.
Visit parentprojectmd.org ↗
National Organization for Rare Disorders (NORD)
Patient assistance programs, co-pay and travel grants, and rare disease advocacy resources for families.
Visit rarediseases.org ↗
🧩 Autism & Special Needs Foundations
Autism Society of America
National helpline, local community affiliates, sensory-friendly resources, and family advocacy across every stage of life.
Visit autismsociety.org ↗
Autism Speaks Resource Guide
State-by-state service directory, free parent tool kits, IEP advocacy guides, and sensory safety resources.
Visit autismspeaks.org ↗
Understood.org
Practical templates and step-by-step guides for IEP meetings, 504 accommodations, and sensory/learning differences.
Visit understood.org ↗
⚓ Caregiver Respite & Crisis Support
Caregiver Action Network (CAN)
Free caregiver help desk, peer support forums, and practical toolboxes for family caregivers.
Visit caregiveraction.org ↗
ARCH National Respite Locator
Find local respite care providers and state respite coalitions so family caregivers can get safe, reliable relief.
Visit archrespite.org ↗
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Free, confidential 24/7 call or text support whenever the storm feels too heavy to carry alone.
Visit 988lifeline.org ↗
