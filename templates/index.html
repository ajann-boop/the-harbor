📖 Written Overview — How The Harbor Works

Caregivers often hear textbook advice without talking to someone actually walking—or who has walked—the same road. The Harbor is 100% free for caregivers and brings real-world support together in one judgment-free space.

Inside the Community Hub, members can connect in The Harbor Feed with daily planner notes, appointments, and medication reorder tracking, pin urgent questions on The Wall of Seriousness, give kids and young adults 21 and under their own space in The Youth Cove, unwind in The Rec Area, and share usable equipment on Pass It On.

⚓ Why The Harbor Exists

Because caregivers face the reality of hearing textbook advice and not having access to communications with people who are actually living through—or have lived through—what they're going through. Since they're not alone in this world, this platform gives them the chance to experience what it feels like to be able to reach out to another caregiver and have that mental support system above all else—because the world changes when you know you're not alone.
